ChatZone

Delete your account

ChatZone · Operated by Sangava, Pune, Maharashtra, India

Option 1 — In the app (immediate)

  1. Open ChatZone and sign in.
  2. Go to the Me tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account and associated data are deleted immediately, as described below.

Option 2 — By email (if you no longer have the app)

Send a deletion request from the email address or phone number linked to your account, stating your ChatZone username. For phone-number accounts, include the phone number in your email; we may contact you to verify that you own the account. We will never ask for your password.

Email a deletion request

Or write to privacy@sangava.com with the subject "Delete my ChatZone account". Verified requests are completed within thirty (30) days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, username, email address and phone number;
  • your sign-in credentials;
  • your most recent location and your entire location history;
  • your direct-message conversations and conversation list;
  • your block list and notification token.

What is retained

  • Zone messages you posted remain visible in their Zone but are anonymised: your username and user identifier are removed and replaced with "deleted user".
  • Suggestions you submitted are likewise anonymised.
  • Reports and moderation records relating to your account may be retained for up to twelve (12) months, or longer where required by law or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

For further details, see our Privacy Policy.
