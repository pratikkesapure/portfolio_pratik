Child Safety Standards

Effective date: 24 September 2026 · Last updated: 26 September 2026

ChatZone, operated by Sangava, Pune, Maharashtra, India ("ChatZone", "we" or "us"), maintains zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation ("CSAE"), including child sexual abuse material ("CSAM"). These standards apply to all content and conduct on the ChatZone application and related services (the "Service") and supplement our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

1. Prohibited Content and Conduct

The following are strictly prohibited on the Service:

  1. any visual, written or other depiction of a minor engaged in sexually explicit conduct, or which sexualises a minor in any manner;
  2. grooming, namely befriending or communicating with a minor for the purpose of sexual abuse or exploitation, whether online or offline;
  3. sextortion, and soliciting, requesting or exchanging sexual images or content from or with a minor;
  4. any content or conduct that promotes, normalises, advertises or facilitates CSAE, including the trafficking of children; and
  5. using the location features of the Service to locate, track or approach a minor for any unlawful purpose.

2. Reporting

Any person may report suspected CSAE:

  1. In the application: press and hold a message in a Zone and select Report, or select Report at the top of a direct-message conversation.
  2. By email: to childsafety@sangava.com, including the username concerned and, where possible, the Zone and approximate time.

Where a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police or emergency services immediately. In India, dial 112 or the Childline helpline (1098), and incidents may also be reported to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at cybercrime.gov.in. Child sexual abuse material may also be reported to the U.S. National Center for Missing & Exploited Children at report.cybertip.org or to the Internet Watch Foundation at report.iwf.org.uk.

3. Our Response

Upon becoming aware of any suspected CSAE, we shall, as a matter of priority:

  1. remove or disable access to the content concerned;
  2. permanently ban the accounts involved;
  3. preserve relevant information as required by law; and
  4. report the matter to the competent law-enforcement authorities in India, including through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal, in accordance with the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and other applicable law, and, where the matter concerns another country, to the relevant authorities or the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children; and cooperate fully with any resulting investigation.

4. Compliance

We comply with all applicable child-safety laws and regulations, including the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, the laws of the other countries in which the Service is available, and the Google Play Child Safety Standards policy.

5. Minimum Age

The Service may not be used by persons under thirteen (13) years of age, and persons under eighteen (18) years of age may use it only with the consent and under the supervision of a parent or lawful guardian, as set out in our Terms of Service.
