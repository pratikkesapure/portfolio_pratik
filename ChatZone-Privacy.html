  1. "Personal Data" means any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data.
  2. "Zone" means the geographic grid cell of approximately two (2) kilometres to which a User is assigned by the Service on the basis of the User's location, together with the chat room associated therewith.
  3. "Moderators" means the persons authorised by us to administer the Service, review content and enforce the Terms of Service.
  4. "Processors" means third parties that process Personal Data on our behalf in accordance with our instructions.
  5. "Guest Mode" means use of the Service without creating an account.

3. Purposes of Processing

We process Personal Data only for lawful purposes and only to the extent necessary for such purposes, namely:

  1. Provision of the Service: assigning you to a Zone and neighbouring Zones; displaying and delivering Zone messages and direct messages; maintaining your account, username and preferences.
  2. Authentication and account security: verifying your telephone number or email address; enabling sign-in, password reset and account recovery.
  3. Safety, moderation and enforcement: investigating reports, suspected abuse, harassment, fraud, spam, impersonation and threats to the safety of any person; enforcing the Terms of Service; for these purposes, Moderators may access your account information, content, precise location and location history.
  4. Notifications: sending push notifications in respect of direct messages and announcements from ChatZone.
  5. Integrity and abuse prevention: rate limiting, attesting that requests originate from a genuine installation of the Service, and preventing automated or fraudulent use.
  6. Maintenance and improvement: diagnosing crashes, errors and performance issues.
  7. Legal compliance: complying with applicable law, lawful orders and requests of competent authorities, and establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.

We do not sell Personal Data, we do not use Personal Data for targeted advertising, and we do not display third-party advertising within the Service.

4. Basis of Processing

We process Personal Data on the basis of your consent, which you may withdraw at any time in accordance with Section 9, and, where applicable, for the legitimate uses recognised under Section 7 of the DPDP Act, including compliance with any law or any judgment or order, and responding to medical emergencies or threats to the life or safety of any individual. The withdrawal of consent shall not affect the lawfulness of processing undertaken prior to such withdrawal; however, as location data is integral to the Service, withdrawal of consent to its processing will render the Service unavailable to you.

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 or the United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation (together, the "GDPR") applies to our processing, we act as controller and rely on the following legal bases: (a) your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), for the collection of your precise location and location history and for push notifications; (b) the performance of our contract with you, namely these Terms (Article 6(1)(b)), for account creation, authentication and the delivery of messages; (c) our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) in keeping the Service safe, moderating content, preventing abuse and fraud, and diagnosing faults, which we have balanced against your rights and freedoms; and (d) compliance with a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).

5. Visibility and Disclosure

5.1 Other Users

Your username and the messages you post in a Zone are visible to any person viewing that Zone, including Users in Guest Mode. Your precise coordinates, location history, telephone number and email address are never displayed to other Users. Direct messages are visible only to the participants in the conversation.

5.2 Moderators

Moderators may access the categories of Personal Data described in Section 3.3 strictly for the purposes stated therein. We do not review the content of direct messages save where necessary to investigate a report, to protect the security of the Service or any person, or where required by law.

5.3 Processors

We engage the following Processors, each of which is contractually bound to process Personal Data only on our instructions: Google LLC and its affiliates (Firebase Authentication, Firebase Realtime Database, Cloud Functions, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity), for hosting, storage, authentication, notifications, diagnostics and integrity attestation; and Resend, Inc., for the delivery of verification and account emails.

5.4 Map imagery

Maps within the Service are rendered using tiles provided by OpenStreetMap. When a map is displayed, your device requests the relevant tiles directly from OpenStreetMap servers, which consequently receive your device's IP address and the geographic area being viewed, subject to the OpenStreetMap Foundation's own privacy policy.

5.5 Legal and protective disclosures

We may disclose Personal Data where we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation, legal process or an enforceable governmental request, or is necessary to prevent or respond to fraud, security incidents or threats to the life, safety or rights of any person.

5.6 Transfers of the Service

In the event of any merger, acquisition, reorganisation or transfer of the Service, Personal Data may be transferred to the successor, which shall be bound by this Policy or by a policy offering materially equivalent protection.

6. Storage and Cross-Border Transfer

Personal Data is stored primarily in Google Cloud facilities located in Singapore and may be processed by our Processors in other jurisdictions in which they or their sub-processors operate. Any such transfer is undertaken in accordance with Section 16 of the DPDP Act and any restrictions notified thereunder. 8. Security

We implement reasonable security safeguards, including encryption of data in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS), server-side access-control rules, integrity attestation of client applications, hashing of verification codes and delegated management of passwords by our authentication Processor. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a personal data breach, we shall notify affected Users and the competent authorities, including the Data Protection Board of India and, where applicable, the relevant supervisory authority, as required by law.
